📜 Meera's Retired Railway Clerk Story
Meera spent 35 years reading delay notices posted as messy text at Pune Junction. After retiring, she wrote a small parser that turns them into clean JSON. Three commuter apps hit her parser all day for free, leaving her with the server bill.

She doesn't want signups, API keys, or monthly invoices. She wants each call to cost a fraction of a cent ($0.001 USDC), paid the moment it's made, like dropping a coin into the bioscope.
"Nobody pays for a notice I couldn't read. If my parser fails, the caller keeps their coin."
